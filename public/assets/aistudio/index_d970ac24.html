Our History — A Glorious Journey

A proud tradition of learning, service and opportunity for the daughters of Hyderabad.

Government Girls Zubaida College Hyderabad is one of the oldest and most prestigious educational institutions in Sindh.This great institution was established in 1953, when there were very few institutions for girls’ higher education in Hyderabad. The college was founded to meet this important need. The college’s official motto “SERVICE ABOVE SELF” still guides us today.For over seven decades, this institution has provided thousands of students with Intermediate, Graduation, and higher education. Today, our alumni are serving as doctors, professors, scientists, and in key positions across the country, making us proud. It is not just a building; it is the embodiment of the bright future and dreams of the daughters of Hyderabad.

Message from the Principal

Welcome to Government Girls Zubaida College, Hyderabad.

Since 1954, this institution has been illuminating the daughters of Hyderabad with the light of knowledge, following the golden principle of “SERVICE ABOVE SELF”.

Our mission is not just to award degrees, but to nurture daughters who are virtuous, talented, and confident, who will bring honor to the nation.

Here, along with modern education, we place great emphasis on Islamic values and moral training. My team and I strive to provide every student with a safe, dignified, and positive learning environment.

May Allah grant you all success.

Principal, Govt Girls Zubaida College Hyderabad
